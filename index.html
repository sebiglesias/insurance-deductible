Frequently Asked Questions

How often does the average American file an auto insurance claim?

According to the Insurance Information Institute (III), the average private passenger car driver files a collision claim once every 10 to 12 years and a comprehensive claim once every 17 years.

What if I don't have $1,000 in savings?

Do not raise your deductible if paying $1,000 out-of-pocket would force you to take on high-interest credit card debt or delay vehicle repairs required to commute to work.

Should I increase my comprehensive or collision deductible?

Comprehensive (theft, hail, deer, cracked windshield) is generally cheaper to keep at a lower deductible ($250-$500), while collision deductible can comfortably be raised to $1,000 for substantial premium discounts.
